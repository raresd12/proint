What is 3x3 Basketball?

3x3 basketball is the fastest, most intense version of the game.

  • Fast-paced: Half-court action with non-stop scoring opportunities.
  • Physical: Intense defense and contact in a compact space.
  • High-skill: Pure basketball IQ, shooting, and one-on-one mastery.

Key Differences

More space means faster decisions. Tougher one-on-one battles. Every possession counts.
